The Hidden Factory: what your plant could run versus what it does

Most plants measure themselves against a standard that someone set years ago. This paper explains why that standard can hide capacity you have already paid for, and how a structured method finds it.

  • The hidden factory. How accepted limits get locked in by standards, costing, OEE and budgets.
  • A real case. A brewery's "physical limit" on the filler turned out to be an undersized fill tube. Resizing it raised output by 20%.
  • Variance Tree Analysis. A scientific, step-by-step method that replaces brainstorming with systematic elimination.
  • Training for your team. How continuous improvement teams learn to run the method themselves.

By Trevor Cusworth, founder of Plantworks. 47 years in process manufacturing.
