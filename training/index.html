01 / How a ceiling gets built

A number nobody questions, written into four systems.

Somewhere, a team tried to run faster and hit a defect. The obvious fixes did not work, so the speed they could reach became "the maximum."

Nobody is lying and nobody is hiding anything. The number simply stopped being a hypothesis and started being a fact, because it then spread into the systems the plant runs on.

  • 01StandardsSchedules, labor plans and delivery commitments are built on it.
  • 02CostingStandard cost per unit is calculated from it, so variances are measured against it.
  • 03OEEPerformance is scored against it, so at the standard the line looks on target.
  • 04BudgetsAnnual targets are set on last year's actuals, which came from it.
Challenging the number now means challenging four systems at once.So the rational response is to leave it alone.
02 / The hidden factory

03 / The method

Don't brainstorm the cause. Measure it.

Zero Loss Analysis starts by asking whether a limit is real. Variance Tree Analysis, one of its tools, breaks the problem into the factors that physically control it, measures each one, and isolates the cause.

  1. State the gapA number, not a feeling.
  2. List the factorsEverything that physically controls the result.
  3. Define expected behaviorWhat each factor should be at the target rate.
  4. MeasureCompare actual with expected, factor by factor.
  5. Eliminate what matchesSet aside the factors that behave as expected.
  6. Drill downBreak the one that differs into its own factors.
  7. Fix the cause, reset the standardSo the system stops enforcing the old limit.

04 / What your team learns

A method your own people can run on your own constraint.

01

Find the real limits

Separate the limits that have been tested from the ones that were inherited, and know which to reopen first.

02

Read performance against capability

Measure the line against what the equipment can do, not only against the standard.

03

Build a variance tree

Decompose a hard problem into measurable factors, test each one, and drill down to a cause you can act on.

04

Make the gain stick

Reset standards, report the gap as well as the score, and bring the result into capital planning.

Format, dates and group size are agreed on a call, so the training fits your site and your constraint.
