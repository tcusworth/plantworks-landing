Where’s Your Herbie?

Your plant is probably not maxed out.

Most plants are running their binding constraint at 60 to 80% of what it can already sustain. The growth the business is asking for is usually already inside the fence. Nobody has measured it, so nobody can see it.

Where’s Your Herbie? is a Theory of Constraints book for manufacturing plant operations. It is about finding the one station that sets your plant’s rate, getting everything out of it before you spend a penny on capacity, and holding the gains once they arrive.

Who is writing this

Trevor Cusworth has spent forty-seven years in manufacturing operations, across automotive, consumer packaged goods, food and beverage, pulp and paper, chemicals, and oil and gas. He has held senior roles at Schneider Electric, Invensys and Deloitte, and was CEO of Hagen & Company.

As a Director at Deloitte in 2009 he wrote Zero to Hero, the working paper that introduced Zero Loss Analysis to the firm’s global Strategy & Operations practice. It was later republished in the Operational Excellence Society Body of Knowledge and remains in circulation.

He is the founder of Plantworks and lives in Boulder, Colorado.
